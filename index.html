Transformations

Services

  • Closets & Wardrobes

    Systems built around what you actually wear, not what's been hanging there for years. Everything visible and within reach, so getting dressed in the morning is one less thing to think about.

  • Pantries & Kitchens

    Layouts that make sense on a busy weeknight, where you can see what you have and put it back without thinking twice. Functional, easy to maintain, and built around you.

  • Garages & Storage Areas

    Seasonal gear, old boxes, the things that got set down and never picked back up. We sort through all of it and hand the space back to you, usable again.

  • Home and Moving Support

    Moving in, moving out, or finally taking on the whole house at once. We work room by room so nothing gets missed and you land somewhere that feels settled from the first day.

Our Process

  1. No need to clean before we get there, whatever you're space looks like right now is the perfect starting point.

  2. We start with an initial phone call and consultation to get a sense of your goals, the space we are working with. Its also your chance to get to know us, get a feel for how we work, and decide if we are the right fit for you

  3. We come back to you with what we'd tackle, how long it should take, and what it costs. No surprises halfway through. If the budget only covers one room right now, we'll tell you honestly which room to start with.

Who we are

Sahar

Sahar is a co-owner of S&S Sort & Shine and a proud mom of three. She earned her Bachelor's degree in Criminal Justice, and her career since has spanned automotive, retail, banking, and casework. Every one of those roles taught her the same thing: people open up when they feel heard. That's what she brings into every home she walks into.

She's thorough, dependable, and honest about what a space actually needs. Her favorite part of the job is the moment at the end, when a client steps into a room they'd been avoiding and finds that it finally works for them. Sahar believes an organized home brings a real sense of calm, and she considers it a privilege to be part of that for the families she works with.

Savannah

Savannah is a co-owner of S&S Sort & Shine and holds a degree in Social Work, which shows in the way she works with people. She's patient, professional, and completely judgment-free, meeting clients where they are instead of where they think they should be.

Whether someone's moving through a big life change or has simply had enough of a closet that never quite works, Savannah makes the process feel manageable. She's a natural problem solver, easy to talk to, and unusually good at reading what a person actually needs from a space. She'll tell you the hardest part is almost always starting, and she's glad to carry that first bit of weight with you.
